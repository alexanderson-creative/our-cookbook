Crispy Orecchiette with Spicy Sausage & Collard Ragù

Caroline's favourite recipe to make and share with friends.

Prep Time 20 min
Cook Time 45 min
Serves 4 friends

Ingredients

  • 3 large shallots (about 6 oz)
  • 1 bunch collard greens (about 8 oz)
  • 2 oz grated Parmigiano Reggiano (about ½ cup), plus more for serving
  • 5 oz burrata cheese (optional)
  • 1 lb sweet Italian sausage, casings removed
  • Kosher salt
  • 8 oz orecchiette
  • 6 Tbsp extra-virgin olive oil, plus more for drizzling
  • ¼ cup tomato paste
  • 3–4 Tbsp chopped jarred Calabrian chiles

Method

  1. Do some prep Bring a large pot of heavily salted water to a boil (use a big handful of salt). Thinly slice the shallots. Strip the leaves off the collard greens; discard stems. Slice leaves crosswise into 1-inch-wide strips.
  2. Cook the pasta Add the orecchiette to the boiling water, stir, and cook until al dente per package directions. Before draining, scoop out a few cups of pasta water (you'll need a good amount). Drain pasta and drizzle lightly with olive oil so it doesn't clump.
  3. Meanwhile, start the ragù In a large Dutch oven, heat several tablespoons olive oil over medium-high. When very hot, add the sausage, breaking it into large golf-ball-size chunks. Cook undisturbed until well browned, 3–4 minutes. Flip with tongs and brown the other side, 1–2 minutes. Transfer to a plate.

    Note: The sausage will still be slightly undercooked—this is fine; it finishes later.

    Lower heat to medium-low. Add shallots and a big pinch of salt; cook, stirring often, until jammy and cooked down, about 4 minutes.

    Stir in tomato paste and 4 Tbsp Calabrian chiles (use less if you want it milder). Cook, stirring, until the tomato paste starts to stick to the pot, 4–5 minutes.

    Add collard greens plus a few big splashes of reserved pasta water. Stir, cover, and cook until greens are tender, 4–5 minutes, adding more pasta water if it gets dry. Ragù should be saucy. Remove from heat and keep covered.
  4. Crisp the pasta Heat 2 Tbsp olive oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium-high. Add about half the cooked pasta and cook undisturbed until the bottom is golden brown and crisp at the edges, 3–4 minutes. Add to the pot of ragù and repeat with the remaining pasta.
  5. Finish Return the pot to medium heat. Add sausage back in, breaking into smaller pieces. Add a big splash of reserved pasta water and a few big handfuls of grated Parm. Cook, stirring vigorously, until a thick sauce coats the pasta, about 30 seconds.

    Squeeze in the juice of 1 lemon; stir.

    Serve in bowls. Tear burrata (if using) over top, drizzle with more olive oil, sprinkle with more Parm, and serve.

Happy cooking

I'd give you my own recipe for stuff but I don't measure anything. This one's originally by Molly Baz.

Also, if you don't want burrata this is also fine without it. If you're lazy to crisp the orecchiette, that's okay too. It'll taste good without.
